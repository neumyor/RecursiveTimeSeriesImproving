# Your probes alongside the fixed diagnostics

Every round you also receive the backend diagnostic card: the remaining
improvement the measurements still allow on the standard residual coordinates
(frequency bands, lag ranges, horizons, channels), each with a confidence
bound.

Build your probes to go beyond that card. The card is a fixed set of linear
coordinates; your probes can see everything else: nonlinear structure,
interactions the coordinates do not express, the conditions under which errors
appear, and the model's hidden-layer states. A probe that merely restates a
card row wastes your round. Read the card to know what is already covered, and
spend your probes on what it does not show.
